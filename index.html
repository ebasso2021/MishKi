Made fresh. Made with love.

"Mishki" means "sweet" in Quechua. We blend the vibrant superfruits of the Andes — maracuyá, lúcuma, camu camu, purple corn — with Canadian favourites to create drinks that taste as good as they make you feel.

Every smoothie, juice and bowl is made to order right here in Edmonton.

  • Real fruit and vegetables, prepared daily
  • Vegan, dairy-free and gluten-friendly options
  • Proudly local and community-minded

Meet Sarita, our AI juice expert

Sarita is Mishki\'s AI assistant, with an Italian-Peruvian personality. She knows the natural products of South America and can explain what goes into every glass — like chicha morada and maracuyá juice. Ask her anything — tap the Sarita button at the bottom right of the screen.

Chicha Morada

  • Traditional Peruvian drink made from purple corn (maíz morado)
  • Purple corn is naturally rich in anthocyanins — the antioxidant pigments that give it its deep colour
  • Prepared with pineapple, cinnamon and clove

Maracuyá Juice

  • Made from passion fruit, a native South American fruit
  • Contains vitamin C, vitamin A and potassium
  • Naturally tangy, tropical and refreshing
